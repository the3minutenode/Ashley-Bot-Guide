# Supporters (Ko-fi)

**Supporting the channel on Ko-fi can unlock the Verified Node role**, the same role regulars earn
at [level 11](levels-and-roles.md#the-ladder), with access to `#🔬-lab`. When your support comes
with the Discord reward, the Ko-fi page shows a **"Connect to Discord & Claim"** box.

## How to get the role

1. On the Ko-fi page, click **"Connect to Discord & Claim"** and sign in with Discord.
2. **Ko-fi Bot** gives you **Verified Node**.
3. Ashley removes your lower ladder role (Member, Sampler…), since you hold one at a time.

**Didn't connect Discord, or no role after a while?** Message an admin with your Ko-fi receipt.

## Thank-you posts in `#🙏-donors`

- **One post per month**, for example "October 2026 Supporters (Thank you!)", listing that month's
  supporters, newest first, then "and N more" with a link to the Ko-fi leaderboard.
- Names are shortened to **first name + initial** ("Alex M."), because Ko-fi names often come
  from PayPal and are real full names.
- **One pinned total** counts everyone who has ever supported.
- No pings.

## Things that stay the same

- `/rank` never mentions Ko-fi. A Verified Node from Ko-fi looks the same as an earned one.
- Your XP and level keep counting as usual, and Node Architect (level 15) is still above you.
- If the role is ever removed (for example after a refund), Ashley gives back the role your level
  earned.
