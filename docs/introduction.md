# Getting in: the introduction

When you join, you can **read** the public channels but **post only in `#🙋‍♂️-introduction`**.
One real introduction there unlocks everything else. This is how the server tells real people
from bots and drive-by spam accounts.

## Step by step

1. Go to `#🙋‍♂️-introduction`.
2. Post **one** of these:
   - 🌍 Where you're from and what you make with ComfyUI.
   - 🖼️ An image you generated and like, with a line about it.
   - 🧪 What you want to learn or build next.
3. Ashley reacts with **✅**. You're in.

Any language is fine.

## What counts

Ashley counts the **letters and digits** in your message, after taking out:

- greetings and filler words: "hi", "hello", "hey", "yo", "gm", "thanks", "everyone", "guys",
  "lol", "test" and similar,
- links, @mentions and emoji.

| You need | Without an image | With an image attached |
|---|---|---|
| Real letters or digits left over | **8 or more** | **3 or more** |

| Message | Counts? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `hi everyone!` | ❌ | Only greetings |
| `hello 👋 https://youtube.com/...` | ❌ | Greeting, emoji and a link don't count |
| `Hi from Brazil, I make product shots` | ✅ | "from Brazil I make product shots" is plenty |
| An image + `my cat` | ✅ | With an image, 3 letters are enough |
| `I want to learn upscaling` | ✅ | |

## If it's too short

Ashley replies with a hint ("Almost there! Tell us a bit more…") that disappears after a minute.
Nothing is lost:

- **Edit your message** to add more, or
- **post again**.

Either way, as soon as it counts you get the ✅.

## What happens when it counts

1. **✅** on your message.
2. **+25 XP** introduction bonus, plus the usual **10 XP** for a message if it has 8+ characters.
   Level 2 needs 20 XP, so the bonus alone is enough.
3. You become **level 2** and get the **Member** role. You can now post everywhere and see
   `#🎥-workflows` with the workflow downloads.
4. A welcome reply appears and removes itself after 3 minutes. The ✅ stays.

The 25 XP bonus is **once per person, ever**. Leaving and rejoining doesn't give it again.

## Good to know

- **Already a Member?** Post freely in `#🙋‍♂️-introduction` to welcome people. Ashley won't react,
  hint or give a second bonus.
- **Spam rules still apply here.** Server invites, ads, "DM me" offers and several images with no
  caption are removed. See [Spam protection](spam-protection.md).
- **Check where you stand** with `/rank` or the **📊 My progress** button on the pinned message.
  See [The `/rank` command](rank-command.md).
