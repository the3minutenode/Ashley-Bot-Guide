# The `/rank` command

`/rank` shows your level, your position on the server leaderboard, your XP progress and your next
role. It is the only command meant for everyone.

## Where and how

- Type `/rank` in the **introduction channel**. In other channels Ashley replies with a pointer back
  to the introduction channel.
- The reply is **private** (only you can see it), so you can check as often as you like.
- The **📊 My progress** button on the pinned guide in the same channel shows exactly the same card.
  It works even if slash commands are hidden for you.

## What the card shows

**Before you have joined in (level 1).** A locked card:

```
Level 1 · 0 / 20 XP
🔒 Workflows is locked.
Post one introduction to unlock it and the rest of the server.
```

**After level 2.** A full card:

```
Level 6 · Rank #37 of 412
▓░░░░░░░░░░░ 1,300 / 1,800 XP to level 7
🔓 Full access, including Workflows.
🏅 Role: Sampler
Next role: Checkpoint at level 10 (2,750 XP to go)

Earn XP by chatting in Chat, posting in Showcase, helping in Help, and getting reactions.
```

(Numbers above are an illustration.)

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Level N** | Your current level |
| **Rank #X of Y** | Your place among all ranked members by total XP. Equal XP shares the same rank. The server owner is not ranked |
| **Progress bar** | A 12-segment bar from your current level's XP floor to the next level. The numbers show your **total** XP and the total needed for the next level |
| **Role** | Shown from level 5 (Sampler) and up. If you reached the level but the role has not arrived yet, it says "role coming soon" |
| **Next role** | The next higher level role, the level it needs and how much XP is left |

## Tips

- Rank changes as others earn XP, so it can drop even if you did nothing.
- Levels 2 to 4 show no role line, because Member is shown by your access, not a separate line.
- If `/rank` does not appear in the command picker, make sure you are in the introduction channel,
  then ask a moderator.
