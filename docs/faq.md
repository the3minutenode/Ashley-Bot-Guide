# FAQ

## Getting in

**Why can't I post anywhere?**
Until you introduce yourself you can only post in `#🙋‍♂️-introduction`. Do it once and the server
opens up. → [Getting in](introduction.md)

**I said "hi" and nothing happened.**
Greetings don't count. Say where you're from, what you make, or add an image with a line about it.
Editing your message works too. → [What counts](introduction.md#what-counts)

**Where are the workflow downloads?**
In `#🎥-workflows`, visible from level 2 (Member). Advanced ones are in `#🧬-workflows-advanced`
from level 4 (Sampler). → [The ladder](levels-and-roles.md#the-ladder)

## XP and levels

**What gives the most XP?**
Showcase posts with an image and a caption (25), helpful replies in other people's help threads (25)
and your first introduction (35, once). → [Ways to earn XP](xp-and-levels.md#ways-to-earn-xp)

**I stopped getting XP today.**
You probably reached the **100 XP daily limit**. It resets at 00:00 UTC. `/rank` shows today's
total. → [Daily limit](xp-and-levels.md#the-daily-limit-100-xp)

**Does posting more often give more XP?**
Not much. A message counts at most once a minute, repeats count zero, and there's the daily limit.

**Do emoji, links or GIF links count?**
No. Links, @mentions and custom emoji are removed before the 8-character check.

**Do reactions give XP?**
Yes, once you're a Member: 2 XP for giving one (once a minute) and 3 XP to the author.
Reactions from people who haven't introduced themselves don't count.

**Can I lose my level?**
No. Even if XP is removed for cheating, your level and role stay.

**Why did my rank get worse?**
Other people earned XP. Your own XP and level didn't change.

## Roles

**I reached the level but don't have the role.**
Roles arrive within seconds. Ashley also re-checks everyone when she restarts. If it's still
missing after that, ask an admin.

**Why did my old role disappear?**
You hold one ladder role at a time, and each one includes everything below it. Nothing was lost.
→ [Levels and roles](levels-and-roles.md)

**I supported on Ko-fi. How do I get Verified Node?**
Use "Connect to Discord & Claim" on the Ko-fi page. Didn't? Show an admin your receipt.
→ [Supporters](supporters.md)

## Spam

**My post with several images was removed.**
3+ images with almost no text look like a known scam. Post again with a real caption.
→ [Spam protection](spam-protection.md)

**I'm timed out. What now?**
You can still read. Every removal is reviewed, and the timeout is lifted if it was a mistake.
Message an admin and say what you posted. → [Caught by mistake?](spam-protection.md#caught-by-mistake)

**Can I share an invite to another Discord server?**
No, not even to this one. Invite links are always removed.

## Other

**Where can I use `/rank`?**
In `#🙋‍♂️-introduction`. The answer is private. The **📊 My progress** button shows the same card.
→ [The `/rank` command](rank-command.md)

**Who do I ask about anything else?**
An admin.
