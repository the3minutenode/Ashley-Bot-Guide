# Spam protection

Most spam here comes from hijacked or throwaway accounts: crypto-casino scams made of a few
screenshots, invites to other servers, and "DM me, I'll make yours" ads. Two things stop it:

- **Discord's AutoMod** blocks some messages before they appear. You see a private "message
  blocked" note.
- **Ashley** removes the rest right after they're posted, usually within a second.

Everyone is checked the same way, whatever their role. Edited and forwarded messages are checked
too.

## What gets removed

| What | Why it looks like spam |
|---|---|
| **Several images with no caption** | The classic scam post: a grid of screenshots and no words |
| **The same post in several channels** | Hijacked accounts paste the same thing everywhere at once |
| **Discord invite links**, to any server, this one included | Invites are how most spam spreads. Point people to the guides instead |
| **Too many @-mentions in one message** | Mass pings are a spam pattern |

## What happens if it's you

- Your message disappears, and a short notice in the channel (no ping) says why. It's gone after
  30 seconds.
- Depending on what was posted, you may also get a **24-hour timeout**: you can still read, but you
  can't post or react until it ends.
- Removed messages earn no XP and don't count as an introduction.

## How to stay clear

- Posting several images? **Add a real caption**: what it is, which workflow, what you changed.
- Post in **one channel**, the one that fits best.
- **No invite links.**
- Don't @-mention a crowd.

## Caught by mistake?

Every removal is reviewed by a person, and **Ashley never bans anyone**.

If the filter got it wrong, the timeout is lifted. The removed message can't be brought back
(Discord has no undelete), so just post it again with a caption. If you think you were caught
unfairly, message an admin and say what you posted.
