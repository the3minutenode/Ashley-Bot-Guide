# FAQ

**Why can't I post anywhere?**
Until you introduce yourself you can only post in the introduction channel. Do that once and the
server opens up. See [the introduction gate](introduction.md).

**I said "hi" and nothing happened.**
A greeting alone doesn't count. Tell us where you're from, what you make, or share an image with a
line about it. Editing your message also works.

**Where can I use `/rank`?**
Only in the introduction channel. The answer is private. The **📊 My progress** button gives the same
card.

**How is my rank calculated?**
Total XP, compared with every other ranked member. Equal XP shares a rank. The server owner is not
ranked.

**Why did my rank get worse?**
Others earned XP. Your own XP and level never go down just because of that.

**Can I lose my level?**
No. If a moderator removes XP, your level and role stay.

**I reached the level but have no role yet.**
Roles are given right after the level-up. If it is missing after a few minutes, ask a moderator. The
bot also re-checks roles when it restarts.

**Do emoji, links or GIF links count as a message?**
No. Only real letters and digits count towards the 8-character minimum.

**Does spamming messages give more XP?**
No. There is a 60-second cooldown and identical consecutive messages earn nothing.

**What gives the most XP?**
Showcase posts with an image and caption (25), helpful replies in other people's help threads (25),
and your first introduction (35 once). Bonus events can multiply the rest.

**My showcase post with many images was deleted.**
Several images with almost no text look like a known scam pattern. Add a real caption.

**Do I earn XP for reactions?**
Yes once you are a Member (level 2+): 2 XP for giving one (at most once a minute) and 3 XP to the
author you reacted to (limited per message).

**Why don't staff have a level role?**
Their staff roles already include all permissions.

**Who do I ask about anything else?**
A moderator in the server.
