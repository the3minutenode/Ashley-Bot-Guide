# The `/rank` command

`/rank` shows your level, your place on the leaderboard, your XP progress and your next role. It's
the **only command for everyone**.

## Where and how

- Type `/rank` in **`#🙋‍♂️-introduction`**. Anywhere else, Ashley only tells you to go there.
- The answer is **private**: only you see it. Check as often as you like.
- The **📊 My progress** button on the pinned message in `#🙋‍♂️-introduction` shows exactly the same
  card, with one tap.

## Before your introduction (level 1)

```
Level 1 · 0 / 20 XP
🔒 #workflows is locked.
Post one introduction in #introduction to unlock it and the rest of the server.
Where you're from, what you make, or an image you're proud of: one real message is enough!
```

## After your introduction

```
Level 5 · Rank #12 of 7,800
▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░ 1,000 / 1,250 XP to level 6
🔓 Full access, including #workflows.
🏅 Role: Sampler
Next role: Checkpoint at level 7 (800 XP to go)
Today: 40 / 100 XP (daily limit, resets 00:00 UTC)

Earn XP by chatting in #chat, posting in #showcase, helping in #help, and getting reactions.
```

(The numbers are an example.)

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Level 5** | Your current level |
| **Rank #12 of 7,800** | Your place among everyone in the server, by total XP. Equal XP shares a rank |
| **The bar** | 12 blocks, from the start of your level to the next one. The numbers are your **total** XP and the total the next level needs |
| **Role** | Your ladder role (see [how Ashley decides it](levels-and-roles.md#how-ashley-decides-your-role)) |
| **Next role** | The next role up, its level, and the XP left. Node Architects see no next role: they're at the top |
| **Today** | XP earned today against the [daily limit](xp-and-levels.md#the-daily-limit-100-xp) |

## Tips

- Your rank can drop while your XP stays the same: other people earned more. Your level never drops.
- If `/rank` doesn't appear in the command list, check you're in `#🙋‍♂️-introduction`, or use the
  **📊 My progress** button.
