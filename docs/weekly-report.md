# Weekly report

Every **Monday** Ashley posts a short community report in `#📑-system-logs`:

- 💬 messages this week and how many people posted,
- 👋 how many joined, left and introduced themselves,
- 🔓 how many people are Members (level 2 and up),
- 🛡️ how many spam posts were removed,
- 🔁 how many newcomers came back for a second message within their first week,
- the five most active people this week, and the **top 10 leaderboard** by total XP.

The report shows **names, without pings**. Being active means you may appear there.

Want to see where you stand any day of the week? Use [`/rank`](rank-command.md).
