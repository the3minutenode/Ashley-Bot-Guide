# The introduction gate

New members start at **level 1** with the workflow downloads locked. One real introduction in the
introduction channel unlocks everything.

## What counts as an introduction

Post **one** of these:

- Where you're from and what you're making with ComfyUI.
- An image you generated and are proud of, with a line about it.
- What you want to learn or build next.

Any language is welcome. A short caption is enough when you attach an image.

## What does not count

Greetings and filler words are ignored when Ashley measures your message. A bare "hi", "hello",
"hey", "thanks", "lol", "test" or similar does not qualify. Links, mentions and emoji are ignored
too. You need a handful of real letters or digits beyond the greeting.

If your message is too short, Ashley replies with a gentle hint ("Almost there! Tell us a bit
more...") that deletes itself after about a minute. **Edit your message** or post again and you are in.

## What happens when you qualify

1. Ashley reacts with ✅ on your message.
2. You receive **25 bonus XP** plus the usual 10 for the message = **35 XP**, which is above the 20
   needed for level 2.
3. You get the **Member** role. Workflows, chat and showcase unlock straight away.
4. A welcome reply appears and removes itself after about three minutes. The ✅ stays.

The 25 XP bonus is **once per person, ever**, and it is not affected by bonus events.

## Already a Member?

Members who post in the introduction channel (for example to welcome newcomers) are not run through
the gate, so they get no ✅, no hint and no extra bonus.

## Rules of the channel

- Server invites, ads and "DM me" offers are removed automatically.
- Image-only posts with several images and almost no text are treated as spam. Add a real caption.
